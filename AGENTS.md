# Working on NeuroTabular

Read [the canonical vision](docs/NEUROTABULAR_VISION.md) before substantive work.
It defines the enduring mission; [README.md](README.md) describes the available
product. Operational priorities may change without silently replacing the vision.
Surface material conflicts and respect explicit maintainer decisions.

## Start and resume

- Work only in the checkout authorized by the maintainer. Inspect branch, HEAD,
  version, Git status/diff and untracked files; preserve preexisting changes.
- Read the current scientific checkpoint, verify its bindings and consult the
  Scientific Atlas, original protocols, canonical indices and relevant history.
  When present, `.codex-context/RESEARCH_CHECKPOINT.json` locates the private state.
  Private evidence must remain outside public documentation and distributions.
- Identify completed and incomplete work. Verify process identity and terminal
  receipts before resuming; never duplicate a completed experiment or live worker.
- If required private evidence is unavailable, report the missing prerequisite
  and continue only with authorized work that does not depend on it.

## Research and product discipline

- Keep Product Track and Research Track separate. Protect published baselines,
  historical archives, reserved sources, held-outs and frozen protocols, including
  D37/R38. General research authority does not admit a particular fit.
- Before any dataset payload, reconcile original identity, aliases, derivations,
  historical roles, rights, lineage and required statistical units/groups.
- Before new training, including diagnostic training, qualify the real runner
  and freeze data roles/splits, seeds, controls, metrics, costs, memory limits,
  acceptance/regression/stop criteria and formal admission. Do not change them
  after observing results or relabel a rejected recipe to resume it.
- Pursue useful original neural mechanisms. Compare appropriate baselines with
  paired, reproducible evidence; assess accuracy/generalization and full training,
  inference and memory costs. Distinguish Efficient and Flagship regimes.
- Preserve stable APIs, defaults, valid-input numerical behavior, pandas/sklearn
  compatibility, RNG/refit safety and persistence unless an explicitly reviewed
  change is authorized. Follow [product policies](docs/policies/README.md).
- Document positive, negative and inconclusive results, raw evidence and limits
  in the established private research structure. Use the qualified journal and
  update the Atlas, existing canonical indices and durable checkpoint after
  significant phases; verify evidence integrity and recovery before closure.
- Keep temporary experiment details out of this file. Link roadmaps to the
  canonical vision instead of creating competing mission documents.
- Respect current authorization boundaries. Do not infer permission for commits,
  pushes, tags, releases, publication, paid resources, Goals or automations from
  a successful test or completed experiment. Do not bypass system protections.


## Cumulative operating continuity

New maintainer directives extend the existing program unless the maintainer
explicitly cancels, pauses or replaces an earlier instruction. Do not abandon,
interrupt or restart authorized work merely because a new objective arrives.
Track compatible research, product, performance, accuracy, memory, reliability
and release-validation obligations together in the private checkpoint; coordinate
their priorities, dependencies and resources without interfering with experiments.
Complete safe active work, or preserve a verified checkpoint before suspension.
Preserve completed experiments, negative results, pending hypotheses and evidence.
If directives genuinely conflict and no explicit decision resolves the conflict,
surface the conflict and ask the maintainer instead of silently discarding one.
Continuity never bypasses safety, frozen admission/performance gates, authorization
boundaries or explicit pauses; it grants no permission for system changes, Goals,
automations, destructive actions, official integration, commits or publication.
This rule remains active for future directives until explicitly changed.
