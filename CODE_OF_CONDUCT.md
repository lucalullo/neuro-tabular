# Code of conduct

NeuroTabular contributors and maintainers are expected to keep project spaces
respectful, constructive, and focused on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Welcome good-faith questions and contributions from different backgrounds.
- Give actionable feedback and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licensing, and responsible disclosure.
- Stop behavior that maintainers identify as disruptive or unsafe.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate humiliation, doxxing, spam,
and unwelcome sexualized attention are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Use a private maintainer channel for conduct concerns when one is available.
Do not publish private personal information. Security vulnerabilities follow
`SECURITY.md`.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, reject
contributions, or restrict participation to protect the project and community.
Reports should be handled as confidentially and consistently as practical.
